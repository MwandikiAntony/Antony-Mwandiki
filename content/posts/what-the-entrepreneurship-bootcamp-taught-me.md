By the time I joined the Entrepreneurship Bootcamp 2026, I had already founded Innovant Technologies and IsoMount Tech Collective. I joined because I wanted language and structure for what I had been learning through trial and error.

## Know your customer deeply

It is easy to build what you find interesting. The bootcamp pushed me to start from the customer's problem and describe it in their words.

## Test before you build

A prototype or a conversation can answer questions that months of coding cannot. I now validate an idea cheaply before committing to a full build.

## Think about revenue early

Good intentions do not pay for servers. For IsoMount, hiring companies fund the platform so developers can use it free. For Innovant, clients pay for solutions that grow their business. Clear revenue keeps a venture alive.

## Build with a team

No founder succeeds alone. Mentors, partners and early users sharpen the product and the founder.

I am grateful to Professors Without Borders for the training, and I am applying it across all three ventures.
