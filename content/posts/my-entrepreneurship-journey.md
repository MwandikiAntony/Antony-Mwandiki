I did not set out to become an entrepreneur. I set out to become a good developer. But each problem I solved pointed to a bigger one worth solving properly.

## 2024: Innovant Technologies

My first venture was a technology company serving businesses. It taught me the discipline of client work: listening carefully, scoping honestly and delivering on time.

## 2025: IsoMount Tech Collective

In April 2025 I founded IsoMount to help African developers get seen. Where Innovant serves clients, IsoMount serves a community, and that required thinking about products, trust and a sustainable business model.

## 2026: Sanctum and the bootcamp

On 12 March 2026 I founded Sanctum, a social network for Catholic life. The same year I completed the Entrepreneurship Bootcamp by Professors Without Borders, which gave structure to lessons I had been learning by instinct: understand customers, test before overbuilding, and think about revenue from day one.

## Lessons I would share

Start with a real problem. Build the smallest useful version. Talk to users early. Protect your focus, because ideas are cheap and execution is not.

## Still a student

I am in my fourth year at Multimedia University of Kenya while running these ventures. It takes discipline, but each side feeds the other.
