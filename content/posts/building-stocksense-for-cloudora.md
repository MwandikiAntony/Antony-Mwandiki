Running out of stock loses sales. Holding too much stock ties up cash. Most businesses live somewhere between the two, adjusting by instinct. StockSense is built to replace guesswork with evidence.

## What StockSense is

StockSense is a comprehensive inventory management solution designed for modern business. Using advanced AI algorithms and real-time analytics, it helps teams maintain optimal stock levels, predict future needs and streamline their operations.

## How I came to build it

While interning at Momentum Labs, I began working with a company called Cloudora. StockSense is the software I am building for them. It is my first chance to turn a client's operational problem into a product, with real users and real expectations.

## What I am learning

Good inventory software is mostly about trust. Numbers must be accurate, updates must be immediate and predictions must be explainable. The AI is only useful if the team believes what it says.

## Why it matters to me

My earlier projects focused on access and community. StockSense pushes me toward data, forecasting and efficiency, skills that will serve the businesses Innovant Technologies works with.
