I study Applied Physics and Computer Science, but most of my practical skills came from deliberate self-training alongside my degree.

## The path

I started with fundamentals: a Coursera course in technical support, then HTML, CSS and JavaScript from Johns Hopkins University, and a Python certificate on Sololearn. In 2024 I joined Power Learn Project Academy for Software Development, then the 16-week Full-Stack MERN programme in 2025.

## What actually made the difference

Projects. Capital Bank of Kenya, Afya Diary Kenya, PesaFlow and the bursary system each forced me to solve problems no tutorial covered: authentication, roles, validation and deployment.

## My advice

Pick one stack and go deep. Build things people could actually use. Read other people's code. Write about what you learn, because teaching a concept proves you understand it. And find a community, because growth is faster with mentors and peers.

That last point is why I built IsoMount Tech Collective.
