This year I am in my fourth year of Applied Physics and Computer Science, I lead three ventures and I am an intern at Momentum Labs. People often ask how it works. The honest answer is that it works through a few simple habits, not through superhuman energy.

## Choose what matters this month

I cannot give every project equal attention at the same time. Each month I decide what moves first. Exams and deadlines come first when they are close, then the venture with the clearest next milestone.

## Let each side feed the other

My degree gives me fundamentals in databases, networks and systems design. My ventures show me what those fundamentals look like under real pressure. I often finish a lecture and apply the idea in code the same evening.

## Write things down

A short plan for the week, with a few outcomes, protects me from busy days that produce nothing.

## Protect rest and faith

Sunday Mass and quiet time are not optional for me. They keep me steady, and steady work beats frantic work.

## What I would tell other students

You do not need permission to start building. You do need to be honest about your limits, ask for help and finish what you commit to.
