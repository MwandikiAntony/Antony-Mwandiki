In 2024 I started Innovant Technologies with a simple idea: businesses should have a technology partner they can rely on, not a vendor who disappears after delivery.

## What we do

We design and develop modern websites, business applications and digital solutions that help organizations grow, improve efficiency and embrace innovation. Our services cover web development, mobile apps for Android and iOS, UI/UX design, cloud solutions, IT consulting and system maintenance.

## Why a partner matters

Software is never finished the day it launches. Systems need fixes, updates and guidance as a business changes. That is why maintenance and consulting sit beside design and development in everything we offer.

## How we work

Every project follows four stages. We discover your business and users. We design the screens, data and technical plan before building. We build in short cycles so you can see progress. Then we launch properly and stay on hand to support the system.

## Proof in real products

The strongest argument for any technology company is what it has shipped. IsoMount Tech Collective, Sanctum and the Igembe South Digital Bursary System are real products that came out of this approach.
